🐾 About 9 Lives: Cat Tower Defense

9 Lives: Cat Tower Defense (in-game title: Catastrophe Defense) is a single-file HTML5 tower defense game. You are the Cat Lady. Your cats are the towers. The household's greatest horrors — cucumbers, dogs, the mailman, the vacuum cleaner, sugar toddlers, the in-laws, Karen herself — are marching on the sacred nap spot across 99 waves, and only your feline defenders stand between them and total domestic collapse.

🐱 12 cat towers 👹 11 enemy types 🌊 99 waves 💛 9 lives 🗺️ 5 rotating rooms

How it plays

Pick a cat from the shop, tap a floor tile to place it (carpet is for enemies only), and send the wave. Every five waves the battlefield moves to a new room of the house — your cats are refunded at 70% so you can rebuild around the new carpet. Three difficulties, from 😻 Casual to 😾 Feral. No account, no download, no setup: tap a difficulty card and the cats are released.
