🐾 Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 9 Lives: Cat Tower Defense (cat-tower-defense.com, the "Site") handles information. The short version: the Site itself collects nothing about you.

Information the Site collects

None. The Site does not require accounts, does not run analytics, does not use contact forms, and does not set its own cookies. Game progress (best wave, best score) is stored only in your own browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

Advertising

The Site uses Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and other sites on the Internet:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

For more information, see Google's advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The game is family-friendly and intended for general audiences. Because the Site collects no personal information from anyone, it knowingly collects no personal information from children.

External links

The Site links to third-party sites (such as itch.io). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
